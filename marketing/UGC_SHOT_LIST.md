# Ligo UGC Shot List — First 10 Shoots

**Who films:** Ambassadors (phone is fine) · **Editing:** Cole/Mekhi for brand cuts, ambassadors self-edit Stories/Reels
**Golden rules:** vertical, subtitles on everything, hook in the first 1.5 seconds, real people over polish. Send ALL raw footage to the shared drive even if unposted.

Shoots 1–6 need **no app**. Shoots 7–8 need the **teaser assets**. Shoots 9–10 are **drop week only**.

---

## PILLAR A — CAMPUS TASTE (no app needed, film this week)

### Shot 1 — "What's in your headphones?"
- **Format:** Street interview, 15–30 sec, rapid cuts (5–8 people per cut)
- **Location:** M Street, Leo's, campus gates — anywhere with foot traffic
- **Script:** "Quick — what's playing in your headphones right now?" → let them answer → react honestly ("okay that's actually fire" / dead stare for a bad answer)
- **End card / caption:** "Georgetown has taste. Proving it soon. 🌌 @ligo"
- **Why it works:** zero product needed, infinitely repeatable, comments section becomes a taste war

### Shot 2 — "Overrated / Underrated"
- **Format:** Two ambassadors or ambassador + friend, 20–40 sec
- **Script:** One says an artist, other says "overrated" or "underrated" instantly, no explanation allowed. Escalate to a campus-specific one (the GroupMe favorite, the frat playlist staple)
- **Hook (first frame text):** "settling this before school starts"
- **Caption:** "Georgetown's about to settle this every night. @ligo"

### Shot 3 — "The disagreement"
- **Format:** 30–60 sec mini-story. Two friends, one aux cord, genuine argument about what to play
- **Script beats:** grab aux → play song → other person immediately skips → "you always do this" → cut to black → text: "every campus argument, one app. soon."
- **Why it works:** story arc + identity ("positions, not preferences" — this IS the product thesis in 30 seconds)

## PILLAR B — ARRIVAL / FRESHMAN (no app needed, film this week)

### Shot 4 — "Nobody tells you"
- **Format:** Talking to camera, 20–40 sec, walking campus
- **Script:** "Nobody tells you this about your first week at Georgetown — [real tip: where people actually hang, what club fair is really like, the GroupMe chaos]. Also, something's dropping before you get here that fixes half of this."
- **Audience:** Class of 2030 (they're all watching campus content right now)
- **Caption:** "class of 2030 👀 8.20"

### Shot 5 — "How I found out about everything"
- **Format:** POV-style Reel, 15–30 sec
- **Script:** Screen-record-style montage of the CURRENT chaos: 6 GroupMes, buried Instagram stories, a flyer on the ground → text overlay: "finding out what's happening on campus rn" → cut to black → "there's a better way coming. 8.20."
- **Why it works:** names the universal pain (every org call said the same thing: fragmented comms) without showing the product

### Shot 6 — "Move-in advice from a sophomore"
- **Format:** Casual, sitting on a bench/dorm steps, 30–45 sec
- **Script:** 2 real pieces of advice + "and download the thing that'll be on the flyer under your door. trust." (sets up the door drop — freshmen will remember this when they see the flyer)

## PILLAR C — PREMIERE / TEASER (needs Cole/Mekhi assets)

### Shot 7 — Teaser reaction
- **Format:** Story or Reel, 10–20 sec. Ambassador watches teaser #1 on their phone, films their real reaction
- **Script:** No script. Genuine "wait, this is Georgetown?" energy. Post within 2 hours of the brand teaser dropping (coordinated wave)
- **Caption:** "if you know you know. 8.20 @ligo"

### Shot 8 — "The countdown"
- **Format:** Aesthetic Story series, 1 frame/day starting Aug 13
- **Script:** Phone with lock-screen date, sunset/night sky shot (Aurora energy), text: "8 days." → "5 days." → "tomorrow."
- **Why it works:** anticipation ritual — the wait IS the product (8pm reveal). Train the behavior before the app exists

## DROP WEEK ONLY (Aug 20–21, app is live)

### Shot 9 — First open (the money shot)
- **Format:** 15–30 sec Reel, filmed over the shoulder
- **Script beats:** flyer in hand → QR scan → app opens → scroll the events feed (MUST be populated — verify before filming) → tap Going on a real club event → "okay. see you there."
- **Caption:** "it's real. @ligo"
- **CRITICAL:** if the feed is empty or anything is broken, DO NOT FILM. Flag to TJ immediately

### Shot 10 — First 8pm
- **Format:** Story series (3–5 frames) night of Aug 20
- **Script beats:** 7:55 — "5 minutes" with countdown → 8:01 — reaction to the reveal (face + screen) → "Georgetown really went [answer] tonight??" → "tomorrow's question is already up"
- **Why it works:** this is the retention loop on camera. First public proof that the nightly ritual exists
