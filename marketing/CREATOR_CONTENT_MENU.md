# Ligo Creator Content Menu

**For:** hired UGC · campus creators · hybrids  
**Ambassadors (distribution):** phones first — they only pull from this menu if they want optional content  
**Use:** weekly pick list — not a rigid calendar  

**Spine (every explainer / closer lands here):**

> Georgetown-only. What’s happening on campus. Music every night at 8. Dropping at move-in.

**Honesty rule (form ≠ event):** You can show what the product *looks like* and how it *will work* (UI preview, trailer, future-tense walkthrough). You cannot post as if the live campus ritual already ran (“Georgetown went X tonight,” fake reveal reactions, “everyone’s on it”). Demo/preview framing is required whenever a screen is shown pre-drop.

**Never as headline pre-drop:** dating / Spark / Sound Pact soulmate energy · Connection Night as the pitch · archetypes/rarity deep-dives · feature tours · empty feeds · paid-per-install schemes

**Physical default:** question posters (huge type, one question, QR, date) + freshman door slips — not generic “download our app” flyers.

---

## Roles (don’t force everyone into the same job)

| Role | Job | Pulls from menu? |
|---|---|---|
| **Hired UGC / campus creators** | Short-form product + culture content | Yes — primary |
| **Cluster ambassadors** | Phones: club meetings, floors, GroupMes, doors | Optional light Stories only |
| **Org partners** | Onboard club + real events | Co-branded posts with Micah |
| **Hybrids** | Ground by day, menu by night | Yes |

Pay for **deliverables / access / events** — never cash-per-download.

## How to use this

### Pre-drop weekly mix (default) — ~30% product clarity
Pick pieces that hit this balance:

| Column | Role | Pick |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Question-adjacent** | Reach + habit preview | **2** |
| **B — Street / product idea** | Proof the desire is real | **1** |
| **C — Why it makes sense** | Premise / problem → product | **1** |
| **D — Explainer + what’s coming** | Clarity / urgency | **2** (at least one real explainer: D1–D4) |

Stories: 3–5 light cuts / reactions / countdown on top.

### Drop week mix
- **2** from **E — First use** (app live, feed populated)
- **1** from **A** or **B**
- **1** from **D** (“it’s here” / D8)
- Ground shifts separate — still post the footage

### Post-drop / habit mix (week 2+)
- **2** ritual / reveal (E3–E4 energy)
- **1** events / RSVP proof
- **1** free from A–C  
Kill premiere language.

## Quick quality bar

**Ship it if:** hook in 1.5s · student voice · spine or clean slice · vertical + captions · preview labeled if pre-drop UI  

**Kill it if:** empty app · dating as main joke · “everyone’s downloading” with no proof · 60s feature list · fake live reveal · Spark/CN as the pitch
