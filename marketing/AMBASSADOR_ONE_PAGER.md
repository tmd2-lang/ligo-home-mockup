## Interview filter (10-min call, ask these)

1. Show me something you posted that popped. Why do you think it worked?
2. Walk up to a stranger at Leo's — what do you say to get them talking about music?
3. What's your circle on campus? (listen for density: team, floor, club, GroupMes)
4. Are you here Aug 17–24? (hard requirement)

Green flags: answers fast, laughs easy, names specific people/places.
Red flags: asks about follower requirements, wants a "brand deal," vague about who they know.
